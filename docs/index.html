      About

      How it works

      The data

      Official monthly recreation visits for all 63 national parks, 1979–2025, from the NPS Visitor Use Statistics data package (public domain). Years before a park’s national park designation count its earlier status, such as a national monument.

      The method

      Each park’s crowd calendar averages 2022–2024, recent years unaffected by the COVID-19 closures or the fall 2025 federal shutdown. A month’s crowd level compares its visits with that park’s busiest month. “Best time to go” picks the quietest months that still see at least a quarter of peak traffic.

      The caveats

      Visits measure crowds across a whole park, not at one trailhead or on one weekend. Quiet months are often quiet for a reason (heat, snow, road closures), so check the park’s conditions before you go. Off‑season months are flagged, not recommended.
